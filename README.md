Written in 1993-1994, the game FutureStrike was submitted to Epic Megagames in 1994 and was subsequently rejected due to its "similarity" to Solar Winds: The Escape (the first Solar Winds game) already in their portfolio.

Primary Authors:  Chris Tchou and Herb Gilliland

Lead Programmer: Chris Tchou
Lead Designer: Herb Gilliland
Artists: Greg Flinchbaugh (3D Modeler) and Herb Gilliland

This BACKUP.ZIP file (see Releases on this Github) shows the files dated as 1994. 
Other files associated with the game are dated April 1995.
